Welcome to Ws

The old wooden clock on the mantelpiece      ticked with a rhythmic, heavy thud, marking time in a room that felt like it had been frozen since 1994. Sunlight filtered through dusty lace curtains, illuminating tiny dancing particles that swirled in the air like miniature galaxies. On the coffee table sat a half-finished puzzle of a lighthouse, its missing pieces likely swallowed by the deep crevices of the velvet armchair nearby. Outside, the distant sound of a lawnmower hummed a steady baritone, providing a sharp contrast to the eerie, preserved silence of the house.
